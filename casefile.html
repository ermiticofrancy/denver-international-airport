WHY DENVER?

Since its opening in 1995, DEN has generated persistent speculation around its construction, underground infrastructure, public art and unusual symbols.

THE UNDERGROUND

Rumors claim that tunnels beneath the airport connect to secret military installations or facilities belonging to a global elite.

STATUS: UNVERIFIED

THE ART

DEN contains a large public-art programme. Certain works have become central to conspiracy lore, especially imagery interpreted as references to war, destruction and hidden power.

STATUS: REAL ARTWORK / INTERPRETATION DISPUTED

THE AIRPORT KNOWS

In 2016, DEN publicly embraced its conspiracy mythology with a “Conspiracy Theories Uncovered” exhibition and conspiracy-themed programming.

THE QUESTION

If the airport acknowledges the mythology, the tunnels exist, the symbols exist, and the airport itself has exhibited the theories…
